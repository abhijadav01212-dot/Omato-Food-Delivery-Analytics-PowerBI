# Omato Food Delivery Analytics | Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Omato Food Delivery Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze food delivery transactions, customer behavior, food demand, member types, payment methods, delivery status, and monthly transaction trends.

The dashboard transforms raw food delivery data into meaningful business insights through interactive KPIs, charts, filters, and detailed analytical views.

---

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to analyze food delivery operations and customer transaction patterns to understand:

- Overall transaction performance
- Total quantity of food ordered
- Monthly demand trends
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Top customers by quantity
- Most ordered food items
- Member type performance
- Payment method preferences
- Delivery status performance
- Food category performance

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Overview

The main dashboard provides a high-level view of food delivery performance using:

- Total Quantity
- Total Transactions
- Average Quantity
- Monthly Quantity Trends
- Top 5 Customers
- Food Type & Member Type Analysis
- Transaction Performance

### 2. Customer Analysis

Top customers include:

This analysis helps understand high-value customer activity and purchasing behavior.

### 3. Food Performance Analysis

The dashboard analyzes food-level demand and identifies the most frequently ordered food items.

Key food items include:

- Samosa
- Butter Chicken
- Chole Bhature
- Masala Dosa
- Gulab Jamun
- Tandoori Chicken
- Pav Bhaji
- Dal Tadka
- Biryani
- Ras Malai

### 4. Monthly Trend Analysis

Monthly quantity and transaction trends were analyzed to identify changes in food delivery demand.

The dashboard provides month-wise analysis for:

- January
- February
- March
- April

### 5. Member Type Analysis

Customer transactions were analyzed by membership type:

- Gold
- Regular

This helps compare purchasing behavior between different customer segments.

### 6. Payment Method Analysis

The dashboard analyzes transaction distribution across:

- UPI
- Cash on Delivery
- Card

This provides insights into customer payment preferences.

### 7. Delivery Status Analysis

Transactions were analyzed based on delivery status:

- Delivered
- Cancelled

The dashboard shows the overall delivery success and cancellation percentage.

## Key Insights

- Total food quantity analyzed is approximately **15K**.
- Total transactions are approximately **3K**.
- Average quantity per transaction is approximately **5.50**.
- February recorded the highest monthly quantity at approximately **4.4K**.
- Samosa was the highest-quantity food item with approximately **2.4K units**.
- Main Course generated the highest transaction and quantity contribution among food types.
- UPI was the most frequently used payment method.
- Delivered transactions represented approximately **94.25%** of total transactions.
- Cancelled transactions represented approximately **5.75%** of total transactions.
